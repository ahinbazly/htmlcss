Learning Methods

When you want to practice, the tools are already in the player

SubX is not a language course. When something in a scene catches your attention, optional workflows built on the subtitle layer help you move from passive viewing to active practice — on your terms.

Comprehensible Input (Krashen)

Dual subtitles and instant line replay keep dialogue understandable while still challenging — stay in context and absorb patterns from real speech.

Shadowing (Arguelles)

Auto-pause and repeat let you speak along with native audio in short subtitle-sized chunks — practice pronunciation when you choose, not on a fixed lesson schedule.

SRS review (Anki)

Mine a useful line and export to Anki with audio on Android, or share text and audio on iOS — vocabulary tied to scenes you already understand.

FAQ

Common questions about SubX Player

Answers about what SubX is, everyday playback, subtitles, offline use, and optional learning workflows.

What is SubX Player?
SubX Player is a full-featured video player with first-class subtitles and language-learning tools. Play movies, shows, anime, local files, and streams with broad playback support, then use dual subtitles, translation, AI-generated subtitles, repetition, shadowing, and sentence mining when you want to go beyond watching — without switching apps.
Is SubX Player a video player or a language-learning app?
Both — and both are first-class. SubX is a capable everyday video player with serious subtitle tools and meaningful language-learning workflows built into the same experience. It is not a language course, and it is not a generic player with learning features bolted on.
Can I use SubX Player as my everyday player?
Yes. SubX supports local video and audio files (MKV, MP4, AVI, MOV, MP3, FLAC), URL streaming via M3U/M3U8, background playback, picture-in-picture, casting, gestures, folders, and playlists — the playback features you expect from a serious media player.
What if subtitles are missing or don't match the audio?
SubX can generate subtitles from the actual audio on your device using on-device transcription models including OpenAI Whisper and Moonshine (for supported languages and models). You can also display dual subtitles, translate tracks offline, and sync timing — useful when dubs, fan subs, or official tracks do not match what you hear.
Can I use SubX Player fully offline?
Core playback works offline. For supported workflows, subtitle generation and translation also run on-device without uploading your media. Some features may require an internet connection depending on content source and model availability.
How do dual subtitles help?
Dual subtitles put two languages on screen at once so you can follow foreign-language movies and shows without losing context, and connect meaning to unfamiliar words when you want to learn. Offline subtitle translation into 60+ languages is included free; SubX+ expands to 130+ languages.
How do I move from watching to practice?
Watch normally, then use seek-by-subtitle, auto-pause, and auto-repeat when a line deserves a closer look. SubX+ adds purpose-built shadowing and line-looping controls synced to subtitle lines — you choose when viewing becomes practice.
How does sentence mining and Anki export work?
Extract dialogue lines from the subtitles you are watching. On Android, export directly to Anki flashcards with optional audio clips. On iOS, share subtitle text and audio to your preferred study workflow.
What videos can I use with SubX Player?
Your own movies, TV series, anime, podcasts, audiobooks, local files, and streams — not only curated educational content. Supported videos in supported languages can become learning material. Practice Mode also offers optional curated YouTube videos by level and topic if you want a starting point.
What is SubX+?
SubX+ expands advanced capability: high-accuracy AI subtitle models for English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, and Arabic; translation into 130+ languages; seek-by-subtitle; auto-pause and auto-repeat; sentence mining and subtitle export; and an ad-free experience. The free app remains a full video player with core playback, dual subtitles, standard on-device generation, and 60+ language translation.
