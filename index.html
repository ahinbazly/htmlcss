Privacy

Your media stays yours.

Technical accuracy matters more than slogans. SubX processes supported subtitle workflows on-device. Some features still depend on the network — because of the content source, or because a model is not available locally.

On-device AI where applicable

Subtitle generation uses on-device transcription models including OpenAI Whisper and Moonshine for supported languages and models. Your media files are not uploaded to generate those subtitles.

Offline generation and translation

Core playback works offline. For supported workflows, subtitle generation and translation also run on-device. Some features may require an internet connection depending on content source and model availability.

Local media processing

Local video and audio you play, and on-device subtitle generation and translation from that media, are processed on your device — not as a cloud upload for those workflows.

What this does not claim

Not every feature is offline. Streaming, optional Practice Mode YouTube videos, store downloads, and some model availability still need a connection. See the privacy policy for data the app may collect in general use.

FAQ

Common questions

What SubX is, how dual subtitles and AI generation work, and where the free player ends and SubX+ begins.

What is SubX Player?

SubX Player is a full-featured video player with first-class subtitles and language-learning tools. Play movies, shows, anime, local files, and streams with broad playback support, then use dual subtitles, translation, AI-generated subtitles, repetition, shadowing, and sentence mining when you want to go beyond watching — without switching apps.

Is SubX a video player or a language-learning app?

Both — and both are first-class. SubX is a capable everyday video player with serious subtitle tools and meaningful language-learning workflows built into the same experience. It is not a language course, and it is not a generic player with learning features bolted on.

Does SubX support dual subtitles?

Yes. Dual subtitles put two languages on screen at once so you can follow foreign-language movies and shows without losing context, and connect meaning to unfamiliar words when you want to learn. Offline subtitle translation into 60+ languages is included free; SubX+ expands to 130+ languages.

Can SubX generate subtitles?

Yes. When subtitles are missing or don't match the audio, SubX can generate them on your device using on-device transcription models including OpenAI Whisper and Moonshine (for supported languages and models).

Can SubX translate subtitles?

Yes. SubX can translate subtitle tracks on-device. Offline subtitle translation into 60+ languages is included free; SubX+ expands to 130+ languages.

Does subtitle generation work offline?

Core playback works offline. For supported workflows, subtitle generation and translation also run on-device without uploading your media. Some features may require an internet connection depending on content source and model availability.

What languages are supported?

Offline subtitle translation covers 60+ languages free and 130+ with SubX+. On-device subtitle generation uses OpenAI Whisper and Moonshine for supported languages and models. SubX+ adds high-accuracy models for English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, and Arabic. The app UI is available in 30+ languages.

Can I use my own video files?

Yes. SubX supports local video and audio files (MKV, MP4, AVI, MOV, MP3, FLAC), folders, playlists, and URL streaming via M3U/M3U8 — your own library, not a locked catalog.

Can I use SubX with YouTube?

Practice Mode offers optional curated YouTube videos by level and topic if you want a starting point. Your own movies, TV series, anime, podcasts, audiobooks, local files, and streams remain the main experience.

What is SubX+?

SubX+ expands advanced capability: high-accuracy AI subtitle models for English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, and Arabic; translation into 130+ languages; seek-by-subtitle; auto-pause and auto-repeat; sentence mining and subtitle export; and an ad-free experience. The free app remains a full video player with core playback, dual subtitles, standard on-device generation, and 60+ language translation.

Is SubX available on Android and iOS?

Yes. SubX Player is available on Android and iOS. Anki export with optional audio clips is available on Android; on iOS you can share subtitle text and audio to your preferred study workflow.

How does SubX help with language learning?

Use SubX as your everyday video player, then turn the videos you already watch into language-learning material when you want. Dual subtitles and translation help you understand; seek-by-subtitle, auto-pause, auto-repeat, and shadowing support listening practice; sentence mining and Anki export help you remember useful lines from authentic content.
